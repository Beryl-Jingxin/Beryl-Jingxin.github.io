Profile

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Institute of Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I work at the interface of molecular spectroscopy, scientific computing, and computational spectral analysis.

My research examines how molecular data quality, spectral resolution, and physical model assumptions affect the information extracted from complex spectra. I developed ExoMolHR and PyExoCross during my doctoral research with the ExoMol group at University College London.

My current work studies component identification and parameter inference in strongly blended atomic and molecular spectra. I am extending this work toward exoplanet atmospheric modelling and inference.
